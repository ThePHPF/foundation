## Discussion Details

- **Website and Strategy Updates:** Elizabeth Barron reported that strategy deliverables are in progress, but the PHP Foundation website refresh remains pending and is scheduled for completion during a "No Meeting Friday". The proposed website changes are minor, focusing on removing date dependencies and clarifying existing content.

- **PHP at Scale Podcast:** Ben Marks is hosting the "PHP at Scale" podcast and has secured the domain phpatscale.com. The podcast has a spreadsheet of potential guests, and the first guest is Passcreator; while there is interest in having Nils Adermann and Jordy on the show, they will not be the initial guests.

- **Onboarding Initiative:** The launch of the onboarding initiative is scheduled for this week, with the first meetings planned for August. The goal is to enhance the newcomer experience by consolidating educational resources and establishing a clear path for those learning PHP. There are currently approximately 140 individuals registered through the interest form.

- **PHP Ambassador Initiative:** James Seconde has created a draft for a speaker pool and defined an official "PHP Ambassador" branding, which involves a vetting process. This initiative is currently in the discussion phase, and final approval will be required from the Board before it is officially launched.

- **FrOScon Booth Planning:** Benjamin Eberlei inquired about staffing a PHP Foundation booth at FrOScon and whether potential ambassadors could assist. Elizabeth Barron confirmed she would provide the booth materials and directed Benjamin Eberlei to the dedicated PHP ambassador channels within the PHP Foundation Discord for coordination.

- **Ambassador Meeting Logistics:** Elizabeth Barron noted that the first ambassador meeting nearly reached the Google Meet capacity limit of 150 participants. To address engagement challenges and invite management, Elizabeth Barron plans to propose splitting the group into smaller, specific sections—research, marketing, and speakers—during the next meeting.

- **Quarterly Reports and Fundraising:** Elizabeth Barron confirmed that the quarterly report will be published today, with the inclusion of suggestions from Matthew Weier O'Phinney regarding leadership-level updates. The "elephpant" fundraising project will be discussed separately.

- **Performance Expectations and Developer Roles:** The group discussed the lack of clear performance expectations for contributors. Roman Pronskiy noted that initial agreements were broad, focusing on "meaningful" work, which has led to ambiguity. Josepha Haden Chomphosy argued that providing a quick "no" to projects is more efficient than a vague "maybe," as it avoids wasting contributors' time on efforts that lack foundation support.

- **Organizational Policy and Technical Steering:** Matt Stauffer cautioned against creating "organizational scar tissue" by implementing policies that only address one individual's situation rather than general team needs Nils Adermann expressed skepticism about forming a formal "Technical Steering Committee" within the PHP Foundation, noting that while the PHP project needs a vision, the foundation might not be the correct entity to define it.

- **Project Roadmap Discussions:** The group debated the necessity of a formal roadmap. Sara advocated for creating a high-level roadmap to guide foundation developers toward common goals. Conversely, Benjamin Eberlei and Nils Adermann expressed concern that a rigid roadmap might discourage the flexibility that currently allows contributors to work on projects that interest them.

- **Metrics and Infrastructure Improvements:** Elizabeth Barron highlighted the importance of using data to identify priorities, such as managing the backlog of code reviews. Matt Stauffer suggested building infrastructure and tooling to track "what if" scenarios, which would help clarify where the team needs assistance and identify if specific issues are blocked or abandoned.

- **Hiring and Annual Evaluation Process:** Nils Adermann proposed evaluating the current team in the fall to ensure alignment with the foundation's priorities, as this criteria had not previously existed. Elizabeth Barron committed to creating clear success criteria for all developers to ensure that expectations are defined in advance rather than presented at the end of a cycle.

- **Meeting Conclusion and Asynchronous Follow-up:** As the meeting time concluded, Nils Adermann noted that the group did not discuss the "elephpants". Elizabeth Barron suggested that this topic could be managed asynchronously.
