Elizabeth Barron intends to continue these discussions with Tiffany. 
- PHPF Website Content Refresh: Elizabeth Barron presented a request for feedback on the Foundation website content refresh, which focuses on updating the content to be more generic to reduce the need for frequent updates and improve overall documentation.
- PHP Ambassador Program Launch: The PHP Ambassador Program is launching with 120 interested participants, of whom 67 confirmed their attendance. The meeting format will be adapted to manage this group by organizing them into subgroups focused on research, messaging, and speaker resources. James Seconde will assist with the public-facing speaker group.
- Quarterly Advisory Board Meeting: Elizabeth Barron updated the team on the quarterly advisory board meeting, which involved sponsors and contractors. The meeting revealed a need to better highlight the value proposition for sponsors, as they expressed interest in connecting with other sponsors as well as interacting with contractors. There is an ongoing effort to balance sponsor feedback with the necessity of maintaining independent control over developer tasks.
- Project Phathom Status: Elizabeth Barron announced that the Phathom project, an initiative aimed at building a parser to provide context and documentation across the PHP codebase, has been placed on hold. The decision followed challenges regarding a lack of clear deliverables and difficulties in communicating the project's value. Joe will complete a final blog post from prior research efforts before revisiting Phathom status.
- Website Operations and Datetime Bugs: Derick is managing datetime bug fixes and facilitating coordination with Mark Randall on php.net website changes, with some progress noted. Additionally, the contracts for the transition to Bunny have been signed.
- Other Team and Staffing Updates: Elizabeth shared various Team updates, including Gina closing stale pull requests and issues, and the upcoming leave for Saki. To manage the increasing workload for Ilija, the team is exploring the possibility of having a sponsor donate an employee's time or incorporating their work into the budget. Ben's contract has been finalized, and the team will begin issuing quarterly progress reports.
- Documentation and Communication: Elizabeth Barron is migrating documentation of Foundation operations from a Foundation Google drive to GitHub to increase transparency and accessibility. A calendar for upcoming events and professional engagements, including Elizabeth Barron's keynotes and travel, will also be maintained on GitHub.
- Maintenance Expectations and Data: Nils Adermann raised a question regarding the enforcement of the expectation that contractors spend 50% of their time on maintenance. Elizabeth Barron noted the need to collect more data to verify this, as unplanned urgent tasks sometimes disrupt planned maintenance efforts.
- Library Component Policy: Sara Golemon confirmed that existing internal policy allows for the integration of library and framework components into the PHP.net website, addressing prior confusion regarding the project's scope.
- Shrinking Contributor Pool: Elizabeth Barron identified a critical concern regarding the dwindling pool of contributors, noting that essential maintenance tasks are becoming unsustainable due to an over-reliance on a small number of individuals.
- Unbundling Extensions: To mitigate the maintenance burden, Sebastian Bergmann and Sara Golemon proposed unbundling less-maintained extensions, such as SNMP, from the core code. This strategy aims to reduce the security surface area and encourage users to utilize independent packages, similar to current database management practices.
- Engaging Distribution Maintainers: The team discussed the importance of engaging distribution maintainers to support the unbundling of extensions, as they play a significant role in the PHP build process.
- Code Ownership Culture: Sara Golemon and Nils Adermann addressed the need for better cultural norms regarding "code ownership" to ensure it does not function as an unintentional barrier to contributions. The team acknowledged that while individual contributors are vital to the project, their roles as gatekeepers may discourage others from submitting pull requests.
- Growing the Contributor Pool: Matt Stauffer suggested collaborating with community figures to help demystify the contribution process and encourage new members. Elizabeth Barron plans to coordinate with PHP Architect to establish office hours focused on teaching community members how to contribute to the project.
